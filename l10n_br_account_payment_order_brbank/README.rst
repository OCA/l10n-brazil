Sends Brazilian payment orders to the bank through its API, using the
`brbank <https://pypi.org/project/brbank/>`__ library and the bank
connection of ``l10n_br_bank_connection``. It is the Brazilian provider
of ``account_payment_order_api`` (OCA/bank-payment).

Each payment line goes in a bank batch of its kind:

- **Boleto** and **guia** (utility bills and taxes with a barcode): from
  the barcode or the digitable line of the line;
- **Pix**: to the Pix key of the line, or to the bank account of the
  supplier;
- **Pix copy and paste**: from the code of the line, also copied from
  the payment schedule;
- **TED**: to the bank account of the supplier.

The banks are the payment adapters brbank registers, and each one
declares what it pays: a line of another kind stops the order with a
message before anything goes to the bank. The bank answers line by line;
the status, the reason and the bank authentication show on the order,
and the payment of a line is posted only when the bank reports it as
paid.

What sending does depends on the bank, and the button says it before the
call:

- **Banco do Brasil** and **Itaú**: the payments wait for an approver in
  the bank channel; Odoo follows the approval by checking the bank.
- **Santander**: the bank validates and holds the payments; the money
  moves with **Authorize at the Bank**, for accounting managers, with
  the user and date in the chatter.
- **Bradesco** and **Sicoob**: the money leaves when the bank receives
  the payment.
- **Inter**: it depends on the approval setting of the account at the
  bank.

Where the Odoo click can move the money (Santander, Bradesco, Sicoob and
Inter), the mode always requires the second factor of
``account_payment_order_api``; at Banco do Brasil and Itaú it is
optional.

Banks without batches get one call per line. A line without an answer
becomes **Unknown** and the next ones are not sent: they are refused as
not sent and can go in a new order. An unknown line is never sent again;
Odoo asks the bank for it, and when there is no identifier to ask (a TED
at Bradesco) it stays unknown for a check against the bank statement.

Usage
=====

1. Set up the bank connection (Accounting > Configuration > Bank
   Connections), with the credentials of the bank API application and
   the certificate the bank asks for. The **Payment Settings** page
   lists the keys of the payment API of that bank; the document of the
   holder, the company name and, at Bradesco, the debit account come
   from the company and the journal.
2. Create an outbound payment mode with **Bank API Provider** set to
   brbank and the bank connection (and, at Banco do Brasil, the payment
   contract and the kind of payments). Its bank journal must have the
   company bank account with branch and account digit.
3. Use payment orders of this mode as usual, then **Send to Bank**. Odoo
   checks the bank every 10 minutes, or at once with **Check Bank Now**.
