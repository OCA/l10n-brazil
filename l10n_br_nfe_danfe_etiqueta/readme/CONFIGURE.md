The label takes its options from the DANFE profile of the company, in
*Fiscal \> Configuration \> NF-e \> DANFE Profiles*, section *Simplified
DANFE - Label*:

- the paper size, 100 x 150 mm by default (the NT 2020.004 requires at
  least 55 mm of width) and the margins;
- whether to print the total of the NF-e (optional since the NT 2020.004
  v1.10), the delivery address and the list of items.

The font family is the one of the profile.
