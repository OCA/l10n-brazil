Connects the fiscal documents of ``l10n_br_fiscal_edi`` to the invoices
of ``l10n_br_account``, in both senses.

**Received documents.** A fiscal document imported from a file stays a
draft document: nothing is payable yet. The button **Generate Invoice**
creates the vendor bill, and only then the payables are born. They
follow the installments declared by the document (NF-e duplicates, CT-e
collection). When the installments do not add up to the net amount
payable of the document, which already discounts the withholdings, each
installment takes its share of the total by its weight and the last one
gets the rounding residue. A document that declares no installment uses
the payment term of the invoice. Files uploaded to a Brazilian vendor
bill are imported as fiscal documents, to be reviewed before the invoice
is generated.

**Issued documents.** The installments and the payment that the fiscal
document will declare in its tags (NF-e ``dup`` and ``detPag``) are
shown as a preview on the invoice, while it is a draft, and on the
fiscal document, so they can be checked. They are written in the tags
when the invoice is posted, never before.

Usage
=====

**Vendor bill of an imported document**

1. Import the file in *Fiscal > Documents > Import Fiscal Document*, or
   upload it to a vendor bill with *Upload*.
2. Review the lines of the fiscal document. The page *Installments*
   shows what the document declares to be paid.
3. When every line is resolved, click **Generate Invoice**. The vendor
   bill is created with the due dates of the declared installments.

**Invoice of an issued document**

1. Create the customer invoice with a fiscal document type (for instance
   NF-e) and a payment term.
2. The page *Installments* of the invoice, and of the fiscal document,
   shows the installments and the payment that will go to the tags.
3. Post the invoice. The tags are written, and the document is
   confirmed.

Changelog
=========

16.0.1.0.0
----------

- The installments declared by an imported document are consumed here,
  from ``_edi_get_installments``, and no longer by
  ``l10n_br_account_nfe``. The rule that spreads the net amount payable
  over the installments by their weight is the one proposed in
  OCA/l10n-brazil#5203 for the 18.0 series.
- ``account.journal.create_document_from_attachment`` of a Brazilian
  company opens the imported fiscal document, not the old import wizard.
  Customizations that depended on the wizard opened by the upload must
  be adapted.
- The installments and the payment of an issued NF-e are written in the
  tags ``dup`` and ``detPag`` when the invoice is posted. Until then the
  tags are empty and the invoice and the fiscal document show a preview.
