**Vendor bill of an imported document**

1. Import the file in *Fiscal \> Documents \> Import Fiscal Document*, or
   upload it to a vendor bill with *Upload*.
2. Review the lines of the fiscal document. The page *Installments* shows
   what the document declares to be paid.
3. When every line is resolved, click **Generate Invoice**. The vendor bill
   is created with the due dates of the declared installments.

**Invoice of an issued document**

1. Create the customer invoice with a fiscal document type (for instance
   NF-e) and a payment term.
2. The page *Installments* of the invoice, and of the fiscal document,
   shows the installments and the payment that will go to the tags.
3. Post the invoice. The tags are written, and the document is confirmed.
