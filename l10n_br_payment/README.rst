Base for boleto and Pix charges issued through payment providers. It
defines the fields every provider fills and shows them to the customer;
the providers are other modules.

- The payment transaction carries the boleto data (due date, nosso
  numero, digitable line, bar code, PDF, penalty, interest,
  instructions) and the Pix data (copy and paste, TXID).
- Each charge pays one installment of the invoice (field
  **Installment**).
- The invoice PDF and the customer portal list the open charges with the
  digitable line and the Pix copy and paste (with its QR code).

Configuration
=============

To enable the automatic creation of a payment transaction upon invoice
validation:

- On the payment mode associated with the automatic creation, enable the
  field **Generate boletos on invoice**.
- Select the **Payment Provider** that will be used to issue the
  payments.

Usage
=====

In the form view of the **Payment Transaction**, the boleto and Pix
fields show when the transaction is a boleto payment.

When a charge is paid, the payment is reconciled with its installment
only, so paying out of order does not settle another installment.
Generating the charges again skips the installments with a live one
(draft, pending, authorized or done); an installment whose charge failed
or was canceled gets a new one.

Provider modules reuse the actions of the payment transaction with
another meaning:

- **Capture** queries the status of the charge in the bank. Nothing is
  captured.
- **Void** and **Refund** ask the bank to write the charge off. A charge
  already paid is not refunded by the bank: the money goes back outside
  the boleto.
