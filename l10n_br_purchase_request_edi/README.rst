Link between the import of fiscal documents (``l10n_br_fiscal_edi``),
the purchase link (``l10n_br_purchase_edi``) and the purchase requests
(``l10n_br_purchase_request``). It installs itself when they are
present.

A vendor document with no purchase order may still have been asked for
by someone. The lines of approved purchase requests that have a quantity
still without order appear in the reconciliation screen as a source of
their own, with who requested, the date and the quantity. From there the
order of the request is created with the supplier, quantity and price of
the document and the document lines are matched to it, leaving the trace
request, order and document.

Usage
=====

In **Match with Orders** of an imported document, the lines of approved
purchase requests (or in progress) that have a balance without order
appear next to the lines of the document, with the person who requested,
the date and the quantity. A request of a product with a preferred
supplier shows for the document of that supplier only; one without
preferred supplier shows for any.

A purchase request is not a commitment to the supplier, so **Match**
refuses it. Select the document lines and one request line and press
**Create Order from Request**:

- the order is created by the purchase request wizard, with the supplier
  of the document, the quantity and the price of the document lines (the
  quantity is the one of the document even when it is higher than the
  request), and it is confirmed;
- when the company asks for approval of the order, it stays waiting and
  the document lines are only suggested for it, so the invoice is not
  generated before the approval; when the order is confirmed the lines
  are resolved;
- the document line keeps the request line it came from, and the request
  shows the order.

A unit the supplier declared and nobody confirmed is not converted
silently: the action asks to choose the unit on the line first. A line
without product takes the product of the request line.

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

- A strip of weaker candidates for a document with nothing that matches
  by product: lines of the same supplier with close value and date,
  marked as weak and never pre-selected. It needs its own source type in
  the ids of the screen.
- An order that waits for a tier validation (the approval modules of the
  OCA) was not tested: the confirmation of the order raises the error of
  that module to the person.
- The quantity of the order is the one of the document, which may be
  higher than the pending quantity of the request; the request shows the
  excess as it does for any order.
