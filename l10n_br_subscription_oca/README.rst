This module adapts the OCA subscriptions (``subscription_oca``) to the
Brazilian localization, the same way ``l10n_br_sale`` does for sale
orders:

- the subscription carries a fiscal operation and each subscription line
  carries the fiscal operation line, the fiscal taxes and the fiscal
  amounts computed by ``l10n_br_fiscal``;
- the invoices generated by the subscription (manually, by the cron or
  when the subscription starts) are created with the fiscal document
  type and serie derived from the lines, so an NFS-e or NF-e can be
  issued from them;
- the fiscal operation follows the order: a subscription created when a
  sale order with subscribable products is confirmed inherits the fiscal
  data of the order lines, and the orders created by a subscription in
  the "Sale order & Invoice" mode receive it back.

Usage
=====

1. Create a subscription as usual. For Brazilian companies the form
   shows the *Fiscal Operation* (defaulting to the company sale fiscal
   operation), the final consumer and presence indicators, and the lines
   show the *Operation Line* and the *Fiscal Taxes* instead of the
   account taxes.
2. Users of the *Line fiscal detail* group (from ``l10n_br_sale``) open
   each line in a form with the full fiscal detail (taxes, amounts,
   extra info).
3. Generate the invoice (*Create Invoice* button, start of the
   subscription or the recurring cron): it is created with the fiscal
   document type and serie of the lines and can be issued like any other
   Brazilian invoice.

All lines of a subscription must share the same fiscal document type:
keep goods (NF-e) and services (NFS-e) in separate subscriptions.

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

- ``subscription_oca`` creates one invoice per subscription, so lines
  that need different fiscal document types (goods and services
  together) are refused when the invoice is generated. Splitting the
  invoice per document type, as ``l10n_br_sale`` does, needs a grouping
  hook upstream.
- The *Sale order & Invoice* invoicing mode relies on ``l10n_br_sale``
  to build the invoices from the generated order.
