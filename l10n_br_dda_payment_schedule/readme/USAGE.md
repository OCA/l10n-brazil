1. Link a DDA boleto to a vendor bill in *Invoicing > Vendors > DDA Boletos*.
2. In *Invoicing > Vendors > Payment Schedule* the payable item shows the boleto
   in the *DDA Boleto* column and its barcode, ready to go into a payment order.
   The column is red when the item had another barcode, which was kept.
3. The *Link* tab of the boleto shows the schedule status of each payable item
   and a warning when the amount of the boleto differs from the open amount of
   the item.
