Glue between the DDA inbox (``l10n_br_dda``) and the Brazilian payment
schedule (``l10n_br_payment_schedule``): the DDA boleto linked to a
vendor bill becomes the instrument of its payable item, so the item no
longer waits for somebody to type or scan the barcode.

- When a boleto is linked to a payable item, its barcode fills the item,
  if the item has none. An item that already has another barcode keeps
  it, and the divergence shows on the schedule and on the boleto. A
  boleto that settles several installments fills the first one, through
  which ``l10n_br_dda`` pays the amount of the boleto; the other
  installments show the boleto and stay out of the payment orders.
- When the link is undone, or the boleto is cancelled by the
  beneficiary, ignored, refused or paid elsewhere, the barcode written
  by this module is removed; a barcode typed by the user stays.
- The schedule shows the DDA boleto of each item, and the boleto shows
  the schedule status of its payable items and warns when its amount
  differs from the open amount of the item.

Usage
=====

1. Link a DDA boleto to a vendor bill in *Invoicing > Vendors > DDA
   Boletos*.
2. In *Invoicing > Vendors > Payment Schedule* the payable item shows
   the boleto in the *DDA Boleto* column and its barcode, ready to go
   into a payment order. The column is red when the item had another
   barcode, which was kept.
3. The *Link* tab of the boleto shows the schedule status of each
   payable item and a warning when the amount of the boleto differs from
   the open amount of the item.

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

- Boletos linked before the installation of this module are not filled
  until their link changes.
