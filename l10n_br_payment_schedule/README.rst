Brazilian instruments in the payment schedule of
``account_payment_schedule`` (OCA/bank-payment). Each payable item gets
the instrument its payment mode needs, checked before it goes into a
payment order:

- **Boleto** and **guia**: the barcode or the digitable line, with the
  check digits verified (modulo 10 and 11 of the boleto, modulo 10 or 11
  of the guia by its value identifier). The item shows whether it is a
  boleto or a guia and its digitable line.
- **Pix**: a Pix key of the partner, or a transactional bank account. A
  Pix copy and paste code given by the supplier is read and its receiver
  checked: a CNPJ or CPF key must be the document of the partner,
  otherwise the item stays out of the payment orders; other key types
  and dynamic QR codes are marked to be checked at the bank.
- **TED** and **DOC**: a bank account of the partner.

The instrument goes to the payment line when the item enters a payment
order. The *Read Boleto or Pix* button of each item reads a code, warns
when its amount differs from the open amount and, for Pix, registers the
key on the partner when the user asks for it: keys are never created
automatically.

Codes are checked by the `brbank <https://pypi.org/project/brbank/>`__
library, without network access.

Usage
=====

1. Set the payment mode of the vendor bills (boleto, Pix transfer or TED
   in *Payment Mode Domain*).
2. In *Invoicing > Vendors > Payment Schedule*, items without their
   instrument show as *Missing Instrument*. Click the barcode button of
   the item and paste or scan the barcode, the digitable line or the Pix
   copy and paste code, then *Apply*.
3. The *Pix Receiver* column tells whether the key was checked against
   the document of the partner or must be checked at the bank.

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

- Linking the boleto of the DDA (``l10n_br_dda``) as the instrument of
  the item.
