1. Set the payment mode of the vendor bills (boleto, Pix transfer or TED in
   *Payment Mode Domain*).
2. In *Invoicing > Vendors > Payment Schedule*, items without their instrument
   show as *Missing Instrument*. Click the barcode button of the item and paste
   or scan the barcode, the digitable line or the Pix copy and paste code, then
   *Apply*.
3. The *Pix Receiver* column tells whether the key was checked against the
   document of the partner or must be checked at the bank.
