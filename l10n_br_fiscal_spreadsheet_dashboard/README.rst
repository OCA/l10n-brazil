Spreadsheet dashboards for the Brazilian localization, built on the
fiscal document lines analysis
(``l10n_br_fiscal.document.line.report``):

- **Fiscal Overview**: amounts and taxes of the period (outgoing and
  incoming, ICMS, ICMS ST, IPI, PIS and COFINS, ISS, CBS and IBS)
  compared with the previous period, amounts by month, top CFOP, NCM and
  customers, and amounts by partner state.
- **Tax Assessment Check**: debits (outgoing) and credits (incoming) of
  each tax and the balance, in the period and by month.
- **Tax Reform (CBS and IBS)**: CBS and IBS of the period, by tax
  classification (cClassTrib) and by CST, and the current taxes compared
  with CBS and IBS by NCM.

The dashboards only read data: they are a check, the legal bookkeeping
and tax assessment stay in the SPED and in the assessment modules.

Usage
=====

Open Dashboards, section Fiscal. The period filter starts on the last
month; the company, fiscal operation and CFOP filters narrow every table
and chart of the dashboard. Cancelled, denied and voided documents are
left out.

The dashboards installed by the module are read only. With
``spreadsheet_dashboard_oca`` (OCA/spreadsheet) a dashboard can be
copied and the copy edited.
