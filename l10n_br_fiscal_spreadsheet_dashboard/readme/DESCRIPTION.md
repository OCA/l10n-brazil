Spreadsheet dashboards for the Brazilian localization, built on the fiscal document
lines analysis (`l10n_br_fiscal.document.line.report`):

- **Fiscal Overview**: amounts and taxes of the period (outgoing and incoming, ICMS,
  ICMS ST, IPI, PIS and COFINS, ISS, CBS and IBS) compared with the previous period,
  amounts by month, top CFOP, NCM and customers, and amounts by partner state.
- **Tax Assessment Check**: debits (outgoing) and credits (incoming) of each tax and
  the balance, in the period and by month.
- **Tax Reform (CBS and IBS)**: CBS and IBS of the period, by tax classification
  (cClassTrib) and by CST, and the current taxes compared with CBS and IBS by NCM.

The dashboards only read data: they are a check, the legal bookkeeping and tax
assessment stay in the SPED and in the assessment modules.
