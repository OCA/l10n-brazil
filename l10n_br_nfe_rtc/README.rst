Exports the IBS split produced by ``l10n_br_fiscal_rtc`` in the
NF-e/NFC-e.

``l10n_br_nfe`` writes the whole IBS in ``gIBSUF`` and hardcodes
``gIBSMun`` to zero. That was acceptable during the 2026 test year, when
the municipal IBS rate is 0%, but from 2027 the IBS is 0.05% for the
State and 0.05% for the Municipality, and from 2029 each State and each
Municipality sets its own rate: the two groups must carry their own
rate, reduction and value.

The override only applies to lines whose ``rtc_status`` is
``calculated``: every other line keeps the historical export, so the
module can be installed without changing the documents of the transition
period.
