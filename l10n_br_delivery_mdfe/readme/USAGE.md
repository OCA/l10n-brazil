1.  Install the module (it is auto installed when both **l10n_br_delivery**
    and **l10n_br_mdfe** are installed) and make sure there is at least one
    vehicle registered on the delivery carriers.

2.  On the MDF-e document, open the **Modal** tab, then the **Vehicle** page:
    the **Vehicle** field, which now lists the vehicles registered in
    **l10n_br_delivery**, fills the vehicle data of the MDF-e.

3.  The vehicles of a partner are also listed in the **MDFe** tab of the partner
    form, under **Veículos Cadastrados**.
