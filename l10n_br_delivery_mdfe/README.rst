This module integrates the MDF-e, from the Brazilian Localization, with
the vehicle registry of the
``l10n_br_delivery <https://github.com/OCA/l10n-brazil/tree/16.0/l10n_br_delivery>``\ \_
module.

It adds a vehicle field on the MDF-e document, so the road modal vehicle
data (plate, RENAVAM, tara, capacities, wheel and body type and the
vehicle state) can be selected from the vehicles registered in
**l10n_br_delivery** instead of being typed by hand.

It also adds the list of vehicles owned by a partner in the MDF-e tab of
the partner form, which makes it easier to register the vehicle owner.

This is a glue module: **l10n_br_mdfe** does not depend on
**l10n_br_delivery**, so the MDF-e can still be installed without the
delivery localization and its long dependency chain.

Usage
=====

1. Install the module (it is auto installed when both
   **l10n_br_delivery** and **l10n_br_mdfe** are installed) and make
   sure there is at least one vehicle registered on the delivery
   carriers.

2. On the MDF-e document, open the **Modal** tab, then the **Vehicle**
   page: the **Vehicle** field, which now lists the vehicles registered
   in **l10n_br_delivery**, fills the vehicle data of the MDF-e.

3. The vehicles of a partner are also listed in the **MDFe** tab of the
   partner form, under **Veículos Cadastrados**.
