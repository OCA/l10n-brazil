Feeds the DDA inbox (``l10n_br_dda``) from the API of the bank, through
the shared bank connection (``l10n_br_bank_connection``) and the
`brbank <https://pypi.org/project/brbank/>`__ library. Three paths bring
the boletos registered against the CNPJ of the company, and all of them
end in the same idempotent ``_dda_upsert``:

- **Webhook**: the notification of the bank is validated, stored as an
  event (deduplicated by connection and event id) and answered at once;
  a cron triggered by the call does the work. A new or changed boleto
  comes with its data and needs no query to the bank. A boleto reported
  as paid is read back from the listing and becomes *paid elsewhere* in
  the inbox, with no accounting entry.
- **Incremental pull**, hourly: the boletos due since a week before the
  last pull, page by page, a commit per page, resumable after a failure.
- **Daily reconciliation**: the full listing, the bank being the truth.

The event queue works in blocks of 20 with a commit per block, retries
with a backoff that doubles up to one hour, puts an event in quarantine
after eight failures and is fair between connections. *DDA disabled at
the bank* turns the synchronization status to error and schedules an
activity for an administrator.

Usage
=====

1. In *Invoicing > Configuration > Bank Connections*, open (or create
   and connect) the connection of a bank that supports DDA, such as BTG.
   Its CNPJ is the one the boletos are registered against.
2. In the *DDA* tab, click *Enable DDA*. The bank is asked to turn DDA
   on and the first synchronization, a full one, starts at once.
   *Synchronize now* pulls again, *Disable DDA* turns it off at the bank
   (the boletos captured stay).
3. Register the webhook URL of the connection (*Webhook* tab) at the
   bank to receive the boletos in real time. Without it the hourly pull
   brings them.
4. The state of each connection shows on the panel of *DDA Boletos*,
   next to the CNAB file source. The *DDA* tab lists the last
   synchronization and the events. An event in *Quarantine* failed eight
   times: read its error, fix the cause and click *Process again*.
5. The events also have a technical menu (developer mode) under
   *Invoicing > Configuration > DDA Bank Events*. The periods of the
   crons (events, hourly pull, daily reconciliation) are in *Settings >
   Technical > Scheduled Actions*.
6. Processed events are purged by a daily cron after 30 days (system
   parameter ``l10n_br_dda_brbank.event_retention_days``). Events in
   error or in quarantine are never purged.

For tests and demos, the demo data points the BTG connection at the bank
simulator of the library (``python -m brbank.simulador --semente demo``,
port 8899) with DDA already on. The boletos of that seed are registered
against the fictitious CNPJ 91.300.766/0001-47, the one of the
connection: to see them in the inbox, a company needs this CNPJ.
