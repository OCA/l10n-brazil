The Brazilian tax reform (LC 214/2025) replaces ICMS/ISS/PIS/COFINS by
IBS and CBS. The Receita Federal publishes the official calculation
rules of those taxes as open source (MIT), in the
``calculadora-rtc-regime-regular`` repository: a table-driven engine
where each tax treatment carries the legal formulas.

This module ports that model into ``l10n_br_fiscal``:

- the 43 official tax treatments, with their expressions, loaded from
  the official database (not rewritten by hand);
- the cClassTrib table enriched with its treatment, its reduction per
  tax (CBS / IBS state / IBS municipality), its credit indicators, its
  nomenclature requirement and its legal basis;
- the nominal rate as an input of the operation
  (``l10n_br_fiscal.rtc.rate``), by tax, State, Municipality and period,
  because the CBS rate is set by the Senate from 2027 and each
  State/Municipality sets its IBS rate from 2029;
- the NCM/NBS annexes, matched by longest prefix as in the official
  annexes;
- a calculation engine (``l10n_br_fiscal.rtc.engine``) reproducing the
  official result: HALF_EVEN rounding with 8 decimals internally and 2
  decimals on output, and the IBS split between State and Municipality.

Data files are generated by ``tools/sync_rtc_tables.py``, which replays
the official Flyway migrations and exports reviewable CSV/XML.
``--check`` fails when the committed files are stale, so a CI job can
watch the upstream releases.

The module is a calculation layer: it does not replace the existing tax
engine of ``l10n_br_fiscal`` nor the DF-e generation, it provides the
official rules and values the fiscal documents must carry.
