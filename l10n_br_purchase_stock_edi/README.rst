Link between the import of fiscal documents (``l10n_br_fiscal_edi``),
the purchase link (``l10n_br_purchase_edi``) and stock
(``l10n_br_purchase_stock``). It installs itself when they are present.

- The receipt move is the origin of an imported document line, together
  with the purchase order line, and appears in the reconciliation screen
  as a source of its own.
- A stockable product bought without an order can have the receipt
  created from the document.
- The lots and expiry dates the document declares (``_edi_get_lots`` of
  its line, whatever the type of the document) are proposed on the
  receipt for the operator to review.
- The document line and the bill line are compared with the order and
  the receipt: ordered, received and invoiced quantities, order price,
  and the differences, with tolerances by company. It only warns,
  nothing is blocked.

Usage
=====

When the origin of a line is decided, the line also points to the
receipt move of the order line if there is a single candidate: the
received and not yet invoiced move first, otherwise the only one still
to come. Returns are never candidates.

In **Match with Orders** the receipts appear as lines of their own, to
receive or received. Select the document line, the order line and one of
its receipts and press **Match** to choose the receipt. Other buttons:

- **Open Receipt**: opens the receipt of the selected lines with the
  quantity of the document, and the lots and expiry dates the document
  declares for products tracked by lot, proposed. A move that already
  has lots or quantities is not changed, and nothing is validated.
- **Create Receipt**: for a line of a stockable product marked as
  without order, creates an incoming receipt in the warehouse of the
  company, confirmed and not validated, and links it to the line.
- **No Stock Entry**: only a purchase manager or a stock manager can
  mark that the goods of a line without order do not enter the stock.

**Generate Invoice** refuses a line of a stockable product marked as
without order that has no receipt and is not marked as without stock
entry.

On a receipt linked to a document with lots, **Propose Lots from the
Document** fills the lots, quantities and expiry dates. Several lots for
one item are supported; a product without lot tracking is ignored; a
receipt that already has lots or quantities is kept. The expiry date is
written when ``product_expiry`` is installed.

The lines of the document and of the bill show the ordered and received
quantity, the order price and the differences. A line out of the
tolerances of the company (Bill Price Tolerance and Bill Quantity
Tolerance, in percentage, zero by default) is marked as divergent, the
document and the bill get a badge, and the lists have a filter. A bill
issued before the receipt stays divergent until the goods are received.

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

- The receipt created from a document uses the price of the document as
  cost; the net cost without the recoverable taxes (``stock_cost_unit``)
  is not proposed yet.
- The divergence is not stored, so the filters of the lists read the
  lines linked to an order line; measure it on a large database before
  storing and indexing it.
- The receipt of a line decided as without order is not taken back if
  the decision is changed afterwards.
