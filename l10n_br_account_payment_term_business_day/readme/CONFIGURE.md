1. Load the holiday calendars in *Settings > Technical > Resource > Working Schedules*.
   `l10n_br_resource` imports the national ones; state and municipal decrees are added as
   child calendars.
2. On each payment term, choose the *Business day* direction and the calendar it follows.

One payment term per tax is the usual setup, because the direction is a property of the
tax, not of the supplier: *DARF anticipates*, *GARE ICMS postpones*, and so on.
